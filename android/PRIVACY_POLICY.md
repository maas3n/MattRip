# MattRip Android privacy policy draft

MattRip processes DVD folders, VIDEO_TS structures, ISO images, and MKV output locally on the user's device. MattRip does not upload media files to the developer.

The current Alpha 4 build keeps paid purchases disabled and does not start a Google Play Billing connection. The codebase contains Google Play Billing support for a future production MattRip Pro purchase, but that path remains gated off until production device validation, signing, and purchase-verification readiness are complete.

If purchases are enabled in a future Google Play build, purchase processing will be provided by Google Play. MattRip may receive purchase status and purchase tokens needed to determine entitlement and acknowledge purchases.

The current application does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated user tracking.

If a production purchase-verification service is added later, this policy must be updated before that version is published to describe what purchase data is sent to the service, why it is processed, and how long it is retained.
